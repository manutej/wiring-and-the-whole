The wiring, not the whole.

A large codebase is a module of systems: units known through interfaces, glued by typed interaction patterns. If that algebra is real, you can ship the wiring to a model instead of the source pile — cheaper, and without a comprehension tax. Three depths. No new results on this page.

The bet

Ship generator data, not spelled-out graphs.

Libkind–Myers DOTS: wiring diagrams are free interactions. Composition is pushout along marked ports. Parallel product is juxtaposition with no identified color — the same constructor now in manutej/jev product().
