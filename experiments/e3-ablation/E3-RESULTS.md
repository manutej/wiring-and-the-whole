## Headline
**B PASSES — no comprehension tax detected.** On the wiring-question pool (16 questions
surviving the arm-C audit), pooled accuracy across 2 models × 2 positions:
**A (explicit edges) = 93.8% · B (seed+marking+motif+deltas) = 95.3% · C (interface-only
floor) = 0.0%.** A−B = −1.5 pts: the factored form was read *at parity or nominally better*,
at **63% of A's tokens** (packB 1,316 vs packA 2,089 cl100k tokens). Under the frozen decision
rule (L2 fails iff B ≤ A − 5 pts), the L2 bet is **LIVE at pilot scale** — and combined with
E2's WIN, both halves of the compression bet's foundation (arithmetic + comprehension) now
have supporting data.

## Design as run
30 REAL Fineract `@CommandType` handlers (seeded selection from the git tree; files fetched
from `develop`), regex-extracted to `graph.json`. Three arms, information-equivalence
**gate passed** (mechanical re-expansion of B reproduces A byte-identically — including 4
natural deviants carried as `+ delta` lines, R5 discipline; 2 handlers genuinely lacking
`@CommandType` carried via `-` sentinel; 1 using constant refs). 25 questions generated by
mechanical templates from the graph only, frozen by SHA-256 before any model call
(manifest.json). 12 runs = 3 arms × {haiku, sonnet} × {early, mid ≈ 8k tokens of padding}.
Deterministic grading; no LLM judging.

## What the instruments showed
- **The arm-C audit is the star.** All 9 questions arm C could answer (dep-type, fan-in
  counts, multi-dep lists — all derivable from interfaces alone) were auto-flagged and
  excluded; C then scored **0/16** on the surviving pool, proving the pool genuinely tests
  wiring. Fan-in counts being C-answerable is itself a finding: in this family, dep-presence
  ≈ call-presence, so count questions don't discriminate.
- **Deltas read fine.** The planted-delta questions (deviant handlers) were answered
  correctly in BOTH A and B — the `+ delta` encoding did not hide the deviants (the R5 worry).
- **No position effect** at this padding scale (early ≈ mid in A and B; one C run degraded
  at mid — the floor arm, uninformative).
- **The one recurring miss (Q5)** is a question-wording ambiguity (a 2-edge deviant asked for
  "its injected service['s]" method — both edges are defensible), symmetric across arms;
  lenient set-membership grading (labeled sensitivity, not the primary metric) lifts both
  arms toward 100%.

## Recorded deviations from the frozen protocol (pilot honesty)
1. **Scale:** 25 questions (vs frozen 50+10), 1 motif family of 30 handlers (vs ~30 mixed MVC
   sites) — a deliberately favorable, maximally-uniform family; heterogeneous-site
   generalization is untested.
2. **Models:** subagent harness models (haiku, sonnet) rather than independently pinned API
   models; answers batched (all 25 per call) rather than per-question.
3. **Firewall:** question templates and packs were authored by the same orchestrator (frozen
   protocol requires a separate question-minting agent). Mitigations: templates are purely
   mechanical over graph.json; grading is deterministic; hashes frozen pre-call. Residual
   same-author risk acknowledged.
4. Padding ≈ 8k tokens (pilot-scale lost-in-the-middle probe only).
5. With 16 pool questions × 4 runs/arm, ±1.5 pts is within noise — the claim is strictly
   "no ≥5-pt tax detected," not "B is better."
